Our Story

ABOUT TEDx

TEDxSreyas Institute is an independently organized TED event that brings together innovators, thinkers, and change-makers from diverse fields to share ideas worth spreading. In alignment with TED's mission, our goal is to spark meaningful discussions, inspire new perspectives, and foster strong connections within our community.

Organized by the students of Sreyas Institute of Engineering and Technology, TEDxSreyas Institute serves as a platform to showcase innovative ideas and inspiring stories that challenge conventional thinking and encourage impactful conversations.
