About Sreyas Institute

Sreyas Institute of Engineering and Technology is a premier institution of higher learning located in Bandlaguda, Nagole, Hyderabad. Established with a vision to nurture innovation and academic excellence, the college has been at the forefront of engineering education, producing world-class engineers and innovators.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, dedicated faculty, and a vibrant campus culture, Sreyas Institute provides a nurturing environment that encourages students to think beyond boundaries and create meaningful impact. The college aims to bridge the gap between academia and industry through excellence.

The Idea That Drives Us

OUR THEME

REIMAGINING TOMORROW

Reimagining is the act of dismantling the impossible. It means questioning the norm, shattering boundaries, and shifting perspectives. We challenge ourselves to view problems not as roadblocks, but as puzzles waiting for a paradigm drop.

Tomorrow is the uncharted canvas where our visions unfold. It is the call to push past comfort zones and dare to manifest a world that doesn't yet exist. Together, we sculpt the future from the bold ideas of today.

At TEDxSreyas Institute, this theme acts as a catalyst for revolutionary ideas. We recognize that progress isn't born from complacency, but from the audacity to dream bigger. Whether through groundbreaking technology, sustainable practices, or transformative social movements, we are dedicated to providing a stage for voices unafraid to redraw the blueprints of society.

The future isn't something that just happens to us; it is a reality we design. Every local discovery, every challenged assumption, and every independent idea shared on our stage is a stepping stone toward a more enlightened, equitable, and dynamic world. Join us as we dissect the complexities of the present and lay the foundation for a brilliant, shared future.

Our Foundation

These four pillars form the foundation of everything we do at TEDxSreyas Institute.

Innovation
